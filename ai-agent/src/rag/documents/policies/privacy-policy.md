# Privacy & Security Policy — STM Fruit Shop

## Customer Data Protection

STM Fruit Shop respects customer privacy. We collect only necessary information needed to process orders and ensure accurate delivery, including:
- Customer name and mobile phone number (for delivery coordination and WhatsApp tracking)
- Delivery address, city, and pin code
- Email address (for order receipts and notifications)

## Payment Security

All financial transactions on STM Fruit Shop are processed through **Razorpay**, a PCI-DSS compliant secure payment gateway. We never store credit card numbers, debit card details, CVVs, or bank account passwords on our servers.

## Communication Preferences

We send transactional order updates, tracking links, and OTP verifications via SMS and WhatsApp. We do not sell or share customer contact information with third-party advertising companies.

To update your saved delivery details or request account deletion, contact our support team at **support@stmfruitshop.com** or WhatsApp **+91 9142517255**.
