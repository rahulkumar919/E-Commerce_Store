# Return Policy — STM Fruit Shop

## Can I return products?

We accept returns for damaged, spoiled, or incorrectly delivered items only. Fresh produce is perishable and cannot be returned once delivered unless the product arrives in an unacceptable condition.

## How do I request a return?

Contact us within 24 hours of delivery via WhatsApp at +91 9142517255 or email at support@stmfruitshop.com. Share a photo of the damaged item. We will verify and initiate a replacement or refund within 48 hours.

## Refund timeline

- Refunds for online payments (Razorpay) are processed within 5–7 business days.
- COD (Cash on Delivery) orders are refunded via bank transfer within 7–10 business days.

## Non-returnable items

- Items that have been consumed or partially used.
- Items that show no damage or quality issue.
- Orders where the issue was reported after 24 hours of delivery.
