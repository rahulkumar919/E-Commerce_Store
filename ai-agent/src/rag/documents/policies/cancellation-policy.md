# Cancellation & Refund Policy — STM Fruit Shop

## Can I cancel my order?

Yes, you can cancel your order at any time before it has been dispatched from our warehouse or packing center. Once the delivery partner has picked up the order for transit, cancellations cannot be accepted.

## How to cancel an order?

1. WhatsApp customer support at **+91 9142517255** with your Order ID and cancellation request.
2. Or email us at **support@stmfruitshop.com** with the subject line "Order Cancellation - [Order ID]".
3. You can also track and manage your active orders from the "My Orders" section of your user profile.

## Refund Process for Cancelled Orders

- **Online Payments (UPI / Credit Card / Debit Card / Net Banking via Razorpay)**: The full amount will be credited back to your original payment method within **5–7 business days**.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: If a COD order is cancelled before dispatch, no charges apply.

## Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective, spoiled, or missing items, notify us within **24 hours of delivery** with a photo. A full refund or immediate free replacement will be processed within 48 hours.
