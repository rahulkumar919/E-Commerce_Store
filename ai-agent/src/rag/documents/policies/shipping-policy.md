# Shipping & Delivery Policy — STM Fruit Shop

## Delivery areas

We currently deliver across 500+ cities in India. Use the city selector on the website to check if we deliver to your location.

## Delivery time

Standard delivery: 1–3 business days.
Express delivery: same-day or next-day delivery available in Sitamarhi and select cities.

## Delivery charges

Orders above ₹500: FREE delivery.
Orders below ₹500: ₹99 delivery charge.

## Order tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a WhatsApp message with tracking details. You can also check your order status in the app under "My Orders".

## Failed delivery

If the delivery partner cannot reach you, two more delivery attempts will be made. After that, the order may be cancelled and a refund initiated (minus delivery charges for COD orders).
