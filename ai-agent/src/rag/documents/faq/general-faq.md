# Frequently Asked Questions — STM Fruit Shop

## What does STM Fruit Shop sell?

We sell fresh fruits, dry fruits, cakes, gift packs, and fruit juices. All products are handpicked for freshness and quality.

## Are the products fresh?

Yes. We source directly from farms and local markets. Products are packed and dispatched the same day or the day before delivery.

## How do I place an order?

Browse products on the website or app, add items to your cart, fill in your delivery details, and choose Cash on Delivery (COD) or online payment via Razorpay.

## What payment methods are accepted?

We accept: Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI, debit/credit cards, and net banking via Razorpay.

## Can I cancel my order?

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Once shipped, cancellation is not possible. To cancel, WhatsApp us at +91 9142517255 with your order ID.

## Is there a minimum order value?

No minimum order value. However, orders below ₹500 attract a ₹99 delivery charge.
