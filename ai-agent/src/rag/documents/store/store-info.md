## Product quality guarantee

All fruits are checked by our quality team before packing. If any item does not meet our freshness standard, it is removed from the batch. We guarantee that what you receive matches the product photos.

## Seasonal availability

Some fruits are seasonal and may not be available year-round. The website shows real-time stock availability. If a product is listed as "Out of Stock", it is temporarily unavailable.
