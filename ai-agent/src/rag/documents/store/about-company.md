# About STM Fruit Shop — Company Overview

## Company Background & Location

STM Fruit Shop is a leading fresh produce and premium grocery enterprise founded in **Sitamarhi, Bihar, India**. We connect local farms directly with households, ensuring the freshest fruits, dry fruits, fresh juices, and celebratory cakes reach customers with optimal nutrition and taste.

## Core Products & Offerings

1. **Fresh Fruits**: Farm-fresh local and premium imported fruits (Alphonso Mangoes, Shimla Apples, Dragon Fruit, Berries, Kiwis, Pomegranates, Bananas, Papayas, and seasonal specialties).
2. **Premium Dry Fruits**: Handpicked Almonds (Badam), Cashews (Kaju), Pistachios (Pista), Walnuts (Akhrot), Dates (Khajoor), Figs (Anjeer), and Raisins (Kismis).
3. **Celebration Cakes**: Freshly baked birthday and anniversary cakes (Chocolate Truffle, Red Velvet, Black Forest, Mango Cream, Cupcakes).
4. **Curated Gift Hampers**: Custom fruit gift baskets and festive dry fruit boxes for corporate events, weddings, Diwali, Eid, and family celebrations.
5. **Fresh Juices**: Cold-pressed natural fruit juices with zero artificial preservatives.

## Store Values & Quality Commitment

- **Daily Freshness Guarantee**: Fresh produce is harvested and packed daily.
- **Fair Sourcing**: Direct partnerships with local farmers in Bihar and regional orchards across India.
- **Eco-Friendly Packaging**: Sustainable containers and padded wrapping for fresh fruits and delicate bakery items.
- **Fast Delivery**: Express same-day delivery in Sitamarhi, and 1–3 business day delivery to over 500 cities across India.
