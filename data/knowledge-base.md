## STORAGE & FRESHNESS TIPS

### Fresh Fruits
- Store in cool, dry place
- Refrigerate after cutting
- Consume within 3-5 days
- Wash before eating

### Dry Fruits
- Store in airtight containers
- Keep away from moisture
- Refrigerate in summer
- Shelf life: 6-12 months
